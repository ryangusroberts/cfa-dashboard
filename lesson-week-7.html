This week you install the systems that let the business run without you in every seat. You will document your acquisition path, stand up a KPI dashboard and weekly rhythm, step into the CEO seat, and build retention and referral engines. You will also plan your first delegation.

This week's worksheets

Doc 29 · Worksheet

Client Acquisition SOP

Document your proven path from stranger to client as a repeatable SOP, with daily activities and weekly targets so acquisition runs on consistency, not intensity.

Doc 30 · Worksheet

KPI Dashboard

Track the 5 to 7 numbers that run your business (leading and lagging indicators plus conversion rates), reviewed weekly, same day, same time.

Doc 31 · Worksheet

Weekly Rhythm

Design a predictable operating cadence: non-negotiable weekly activities, a planning ritual, and this week's top 3. Your calendar is your strategy made visible.

Doc 32 · Worksheet

CEO Operating Identity

Shift from practitioner to CEO. Audit time spent ON vs IN the business, claim a protected CEO time block, and filter tasks through the highest-value question.

Doc 33 · Worksheet

Client Retention Playbook

Keep clients longer and raise lifetime value. Design retention touchpoints and a continuation offer, and run the retention conversation at the 75% point.

Doc 34 · Worksheet

Referral System

Turn happy clients into an acquisition engine. Ask at the right moments, give them the words (an intro blurb), and track and thank every referrer.

Companion step

First Delegation or Hire

Plan the first thing to get off your plate: the task or role to hand off first, to whom, and what good looks like.
