Readiness & Kickoff

Get honest about where you're starting before your first live call.

This readiness work sets your baseline: the expertise you can charge for, the market most likely to pay, your real numbers, and the advisors who will keep you honest. Do it before your first live call so you arrive ready to build.

This week's worksheets

Doc 01 · Worksheet

Expertise Inventory

Identify the skills, problems solved, and repeated advice you already have that people will pay for, then distill your top Value Zones and a draft transformation statement.

Doc 02 · Worksheet

Market Scan Scorecard

Score 3 potential markets on pain, ability to pay, urgency, competition, and access (1 to 10 each), then pick the hungriest to validate.

Doc 03 · Worksheet

Participant Intake & Goals

Establish your baseline: situation, income goals, business model, time, constraints, fears, and your definition of success.

Doc 04 · Worksheet

Runway & Walkaway Number

Know how many months you can survive without income (runway) and the minimum monthly revenue that makes leaving rational (your walkaway number).

Companion step

Mentors & Honest Advisors

Line up 2 to 3 mentors or advisors who will tell you the truth as you build, not just cheer you on, and set a cadence to stay in touch.
